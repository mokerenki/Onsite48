## Included service domains

| Domain | Publicly described responsibilities |

| Sales | Lead enrichment, personalised outreach, deal briefs, CRM hygiene, high-intent signal response. |

| Product | Strategy, roadmaps, positioning, market segmentation, competitive maps, feedback correlation. |
| Executive workflow | Morning planning, risk and opportunity flagging, operational briefings, task prioritisation, and supported-channel communications. |

## Explicitly excluded domain

Healthcare administration is intentionally out of scope for this audit and implementation. Healthcare-specific memory schema, policy routing, service registration, task suggestions, and capability exposure must not be expanded as part of this hardening work.

## Cross-domain operating commitments reflected on the site

The public offering portrays secure, always-on agents that prepare proactive morning briefings, surface alerts, draft work for approval, perform research, drafting and analysis, and operate through connected business tools. The implementation should therefore maintain strict permission boundaries, organisation and user isolation, approval gates for consequential operations, resilient connector execution, auditable task state, and service-specific tool routing.

## Connector emphasis

The public site presents integrations across communications, development, CRM, finance, content, e-commerce, productivity, and advertising systems. The backend should expose only configured and authenticated connector capabilities. Any unavailable integration must fail clearly without causing agent orchestration to stall.
